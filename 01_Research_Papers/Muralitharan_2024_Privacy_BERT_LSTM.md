# Privacy BERT-LSTM: Sensitive Information Detection in Textual Documents

## Paper Information

**Title:** Privacy BERT-LSTM: a novel NLP algorithm for sensitive information detection in textual documents

**Authors:** J. Muralitharan and C. Arumugam

**Year:** 2024

**Publication:** Neural Computing and Applications, Volume 36, Pages 15439–15454

**DOI:** 10.1007/s00521-024-09707-w

## Research Problem

Sensitive information contained in textual data can be difficult to identify using conventional approaches because the meaning and sensitivity of information can depend on its surrounding context.

## Method / Technique

The study proposes a BERT-LSTM model that combines BERT contextual embeddings with an LSTM architecture and an attention mechanism for detecting sensitive information in text.

## Dataset / Tools

The research used the SMS Spam Collection dataset to evaluate the proposed approach.

## Main Findings

The proposed model reported approximately 92.50% accuracy, 85.02% F1-score and 89.36% precision.

## Limitation

The evaluation uses an SMS text dataset rather than a large enterprise document environment. The approach also focuses on sensitive-information detection rather than complete DLP enforcement or user-risk analysis.

## Relevance to Proposed Research

This research supports the use of NLP and contextual language models for automated sensitive-data detection. The proposed DLP framework can use similar classification techniques as one layer of its decision-making process while additionally incorporating UEBA risk scoring and endpoint enforcement.
