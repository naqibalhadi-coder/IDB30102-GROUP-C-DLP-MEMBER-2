# Role of User and Entity Behavior Analytics in Detecting Insider Attacks

## Paper Information

**Title:** Role of User and Entity Behavior Analytics in Detecting Insider Attacks

**Authors:** S. Khaliq, Z. U. A. Tariq, A. Masood, et al.

**Year:** 2020

**Publication:** 2020 International Conference on Cyber Warfare and Security (ICCWS)

**DOI:** 10.1109/ICCWS48432.2020.9292394

## Research Problem

Traditional security monitoring approaches can struggle to detect sophisticated insider attacks, particularly when malicious activities are performed by legitimate users with authorised access.

## Method / Technique

The study discusses User and Entity Behavior Analytics (UEBA) as an approach for detecting abnormal behaviour. UEBA can involve user profiling, activity mapping, role-based analysis and risk scoring to identify activities that deviate from expected behaviour.

## Dataset / Tools

The paper focuses on the role and application of UEBA for insider-attack detection. A specific experimental dataset is not identified in the information reviewed for this literature analysis.

## Main Findings

UEBA can improve insider-threat detection by establishing behavioural patterns for users and entities and identifying deviations from normal activity. Risk-based analysis can help security teams identify potentially suspicious behaviour.

## Limitation

UEBA primarily focuses on behavioural activity and user/entity risk. By itself, it does not provide automated classification of the sensitivity or context of the data being transferred.

## Relevance to Proposed Research

This study directly supports the proposed use of UEBA and dynamic user-risk scoring. The proposed research combines this behavioural analysis with automated sensitive-data classification and DLP enforcement, allowing the system to consider both who is performing an action and what type of data is involved.
